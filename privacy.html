Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Streamly Sim ("the game") is made by AetherEdge Studios. This page explains what data the game and this website use.

Your game progress

Your save (channel name, videos, money, settings) is stored only in your own browser using local storage. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or using Settings → Delete save in the game, removes it.

Advertising

The game may show optional rewarded ads, for example to skip an upload wait. These are served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, which may include personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and manage personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Fonts and hosting

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, these services receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your device requests files from them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 5, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
