Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Streamly Sim ("the game") is an Android game made by AetherEdge Studios. This policy explains what information the game and this website use, and why.

The short version

We don't have accounts, we don't run servers for the game, and we never see your save. The only outside service in the game is Google AdMob, which shows the optional video ads you choose to watch.

Your game progress

Your save (channel name, videos, money, settings) is stored only on your phone, inside the app. It is never sent to us. You can delete it any time in the game under Settings → Delete save, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the save in your own Google backup; that backup belongs to your Google account, not to us.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one, for example to skip an upload wait or to get a sponsor bonus. These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, which can include:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was watched to the end)
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and app performance

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it may be used to show personalised ads. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in the game under Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

This website

This website is hosted on Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, these services receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your device requests a page. The website does not use cookies or analytics.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

We don't hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your save can be removed in the game or by uninstalling it, as above. Advertising data is controlled by Google; you can manage it through your phone's ad settings and at My Ad Center.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
